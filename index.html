Frequently asked questions

Is the Starter plan really free?

Yes. Starter is free forever for up to 5 people and 3 projects. No credit card is required.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Paid plans are billed monthly or yearly and you can cancel from your settings at any time. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata.

Where is my data stored?

You choose EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia) hosting when you create your workspace. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes, registered non-profits get 50% off the Team and Business plans. Contact us to apply.
